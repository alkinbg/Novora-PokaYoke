# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers commit to making participation in this project welcoming and free of harassment, regardless of background, identity, ability or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Discuss code and ideas on their merits, assume good intent, and offer actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, unwanted attention, and disclosure of another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), indicating that the report concerns community conduct. Maintainers will review reports confidentially where reasonably possible and may remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Maintainers are also subject to this policy.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue discussions, pull requests and related project spaces.
